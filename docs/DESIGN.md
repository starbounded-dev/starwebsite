# Starbounded visual identity

Starbounded uses a studio-workbench direction built around the provided LuxEngine logo and actual editor captures.

- Violet, near-black plum, and cool silver form the palette. Light and dark themes share the same hierarchy.
- Space Grotesk provides the display voice; IBM Plex Sans handles longer reading. No serif headline treatment is used.
- The home page introduces the studio, then pairs a project note with a complete, unobscured editor screenshot.
- The screenshot gallery uses a side selector on desktop and a compact row on mobile. Full-size viewing and keyboard controls remain available.
- Forward is a vertical development ledger. Current work and long-term ideas remain visibly distinct.
- The footer and contact invitation are compact, with no oversized decorative wordmark or background artwork.

Screenshots and branding come from the project owner. Product statements distinguish the working tools shown in the screenshots from planned capabilities. Design tokens live at the beginning of app/globals.css.
